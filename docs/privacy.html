Privacy Policy - ModifyKit

Last Updated: December 2024

Overview

ModifyKit ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ModifyKit website and all associated tools (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Key Principle: Zero File Storage

ModifyKit does NOT upload, store, or process your files on any server. All processing happens directly in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files never leave your device.

Information We DO NOT Collect

  • Your uploaded files or their contents
  • Any data you input into our tools
  • Personal identification information (unless you voluntarily contact us)

Information We May Collect

  • Anonymous analytics: Page views, general usage patterns, country-level location
  • Contact form submissions: If you voluntarily email us
  • Newsletter signups: If you subscribe (optional)

Cookies

We use minimal cookies:

  • Theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • AdSense consent (Google AdSense)

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdSense: Used for ad placement. Visit Google Ads Settings to opt out of personalized ads.
  • Analytics: Privacy-focused analytics that does not use cookies.
